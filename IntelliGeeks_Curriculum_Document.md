## Teaching Methodology

### 1. Project-Based Learning
Students learn through building real projects and applications, not just theoretical concepts. Each module includes hands-on projects that simulate real-world challenges.

### 2. Flipped Classroom Approach
Pre-session materials and resources allow students to come prepared, with class time focused on hands-on practice, problem-solving, and collaborative learning.

### 3. Peer Collaboration
Students work in pairs and groups regularly, developing collaboration skills essential for modern software development and fostering a community of learning.

### 4. Individualized Mentorship
With only 20 students per cohort, instructors provide personalized feedback, guidance, and support tailored to each student's learning pace and style.

### 5. Industry-Relevant Tools
Students work with the same tools and technologies used by professional engineers: Python, Git, GitHub, Jupyter Notebooks, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, and more.

### 6. Continuous Assessment
Regular quizzes, assignments, and projects ensure continuous learning and provide opportunities for improvement throughout the program.

### 7. Real-World Case Studies
Industry case studies and examples help students understand how concepts apply in professional settings and inspire future career aspirations.
