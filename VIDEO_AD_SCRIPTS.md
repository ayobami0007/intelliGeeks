## Script 1 — Personal Introduction Ad

**Title:** "Meet the Founder"
**Format:** Talking-head video, casual but confident
**Suggested Duration:** 60–90 seconds
**Audience:** Parents of children aged 12–18, general public
**Tone:** Warm, personal, credible, relatable

---

### Production Notes

- Film on a clean, branded background (IntelliGeeks blue or neutral)
- Natural lighting preferred — soft box or ring light for indoor
- Direct eye contact with camera throughout
- Casual smart attire — professional but approachable
- Subtitles required — majority of social media watched on mute
- Suggested music: none or very subtle background ambience

---

### Script

**[OPEN — Tajudeen on camera, warm smile, clean background]**

> "Hey, I'm Tajudeen Nurudeen — but most people just call me Nur."

> "I'm a First Class Computer Science graduate, a Software Engineer, and the founder of IntelliGeeks."

> "For the past 7 years, I've been building AI-powered technology — the kind that real companies use, every single day."

> "But something always bothered me."

> "The kids who are going to run this world — they're not being prepared for it."

**[Pause. Look directly into camera.]**

> "Not really."

> "Schools teach them what was relevant 20 years ago. But the world they're entering — it runs on Artificial Intelligence. It runs on software. It runs on people who know how to *build* things."

> "So I built IntelliGeeks."

> "A place where kids aged 12 to 18 don't just learn *about* technology — they learn how to *create* it."

> "And here's something I know for certain — a great student starts with a great teacher."

> "That's why at IntelliGeeks, we don't just hire anyone to teach. Every single instructor is at least a 2:1 graduate in CS or Maths b n — someone who didn't just pass, they *excelled*. And not just in school — they've spent years building real AI solutions, real software, for real companies."

> "Because when a child is taught by someone who has *done* it — not just read about it — everything changes. They don't just learn faster. They learn *better*. They believe it's possible. And they start building things that prove it."

> "Small classes. Real projects. Expert teachers. And a curriculum I designed myself — from my experience building the same tools the biggest companies in the world use."

> "I don't just teach code. I teach kids how to *think* like engineers."

**[Smile. Confident close.]**

> "If you want your child to be ready for tomorrow — not just surviving it, but *leading* it — I want to meet them."

> "IntelliGeeks. Let's build the future together."

**[LOWER THIRD TEXT ON SCREEN:]**
Tajudeen Nurudeen | Founder & Lead Instructor, IntelliGeeks | intelligeeks.com

## Script 2 — Company Mission, Vision & Objectives Ad

**Title:** "What We Stand For"
**Format:** Mixed — talking-head + B-roll footage
**Suggested Duration:** 90–120 seconds
**Audience:** Parents, guardians, and young learners aged 12–18
**Tone:** Inspiring, clear, purposeful, accessible to all education levels

---

### Production Notes

- Open with a quick montage: students on laptops, coding, collaborating, smiling
- Tajudeen on camera for key sections — voice-over acceptable over B-roll
- Music: Inspirational, builds gradually — not dramatic, not corporate
- Subtitles required throughout
- Program pricing should match market:
  - Nigerian audience: ₦200,000
  - International audience: $130
- Create two versions of the end card (Naira / USD) if targeting both markets
- Keep pacing energetic — cut every 4–6 seconds during B-roll sections

---

### Script

**[OPEN — Upbeat music. Quick montage: young students learning, typing, building, instructor one-on-one with student]**

**[TAJUDEEN — voice-over or on camera:]**

> "Every parent wants one thing for their child — a future."

> "A future with opportunity. A future with options. A future where they're not left behind."

**[Cut to Tajudeen on camera — direct, purposeful]**

> "At IntelliGeeks, that future has a name. It's called Artificial Intelligence — and we're making sure your child is ready for it."

**[Brief pause. Shift tone — passionate but measured.]**

> "Here is what we believe:"

> "Every child is capable of greatness — they just need the right environment to unlock it."

> "We built IntelliGeeks because we refused to accept that world-class tech education was only for a few people in a few places."

**[B-roll: small class, instructor working closely with individual student]**

> "Our mission is simple:"

> "Equip the next generation with real AI and software engineering skills — skills that are being used right now, in companies all over the world."

> "Not theory. Not memorizing. Building."

**[Back on camera — confident, warm]**

> "Our vision? A generation of young people who don't just *use* technology. Who *create* it. Who *lead* it. Who *own* their place in the future."

**[Shift to program details — clear, deliberate delivery]**

> "So here is how we do it:"

> "We take a maximum of 20 students per class — because your child is not a number."

> "We run a 3-month intensive program that covers Computational Thinking, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Software Engineering — from scratch, all the way to building real projects."

> "Our instructors are first-class Computer Science graduates with years of real industry experience. Not just teachers — active engineers."

> "And we reward hard work."

> "Score 80% or above in your final exam? Get cashback."

> "Show up consistently — 95% attendance or more? Get cashback."

> "Because excellence deserves recognition."

**[Music swells slightly. Final beat — warm, direct close.]**

> "We are not just a coding school."

> "We are the place where the next generation of innovators are made."

> "We are IntelliGeeks."

**[LOGO ANIMATION — IntelliGeeks brand]**

**[TEXT ON SCREEN — displayed line by line:]**
- "Training Tomorrow's AI Engineers Today"
- "Ages 12–18 | 16 Weeks | Max 20 Students"
- "₦200,000 — intelligeeks.com"

**[END CARD:]**
- IntelliGeeks logo
- Website: intelligeeks.com
- "Enroll Now — Spots Are Limited"
- Facebook & LinkedIn handles
