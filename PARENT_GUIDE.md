## What Will My Child Actually Learn?

The programme is divided into three stages, each building on the last. Here is what each stage means — in plain language:

---

### Stage 1 — How to Think Like an Engineer (Weeks 1–4)

Before your child writes a single line of code, they will first learn **how to think differently**.

This stage teaches what is called *Computational Thinking* — a structured way of approaching problems. Your child will learn how to:

- Break any big, confusing problem into smaller, manageable pieces
- Spot patterns and use them to find solutions faster
- Design step-by-step plans (called algorithms) to solve real challenges
- Identify mistakes, trace where they went wrong, and fix them

**Why does this matter?** This way of thinking is used every day — not just in technology, but in business, medicine, law, and life. Children who develop this skill become sharper, calmer, and more confident problem-solvers in every area.

---

### Stage 2 — How Software Is Built (Weeks 5–8)

This stage introduces your child to **software engineering** — the craft behind every app, website, and digital tool they use daily.

Your child will learn:

- How professional engineers plan and design software before building it
- The basics of programming using **Python** — the world's most beginner-friendly and widely used programming language
- How to write clean, organised code that actually works
- How teams of engineers collaborate on large projects using professional tools like **Git and GitHub** — the same tools used at companies like Google, Microsoft, and Amazon

**Why does this matter?** Software engineering is one of the highest-paid, most in-demand skills on the planet. Even if your child does not become a programmer, understanding how software is built gives them a massive advantage — in any career they choose.

---

### Stage 3 — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (Weeks 9–12)

This is where the programme goes from impressive to extraordinary.

Your child will learn **how AI actually works** — not just what it is, but how to build it. Topics include:

- What Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are, and how they are used in the real world
- How computers learn from data — the same process behind recommendation systems on Netflix and YouTube, fraud detection at banks, and medical diagnosis tools
- How to build and train their own AI models — from scratch
- How computers recognise images, understand human language, and make predictions
- The ethical responsibilities that come with building AI technology

The programme ends with a **real AI project** — your child will design, build, and present a working AI solution from start to finish.

**Why does this matter?** AI is the defining technology of our generation. Children who understand it will have a head start that most adults do not have today.
