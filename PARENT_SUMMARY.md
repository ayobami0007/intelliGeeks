## What Will My Child Actually Learn?

The programme runs for **3 months** and is structured in three clear stages. Think of it like building a house — we start with a strong foundation, then erect the walls, and finally put on the roof.

---

### Stage 1 — How to Think Like a Problem-Solver (Weeks 1–4)

Before we teach your child to write a single line of code, we teach them *how to think.*

Most children — and adults — approach problems the same way: by panicking, guessing, or giving up. IntelliGeeks teaches what the world's best engineers do instinctively: they *break problems into smaller pieces, spot patterns, eliminate what doesn't matter, and build step-by-step solutions.*

In plain terms, by the end of this stage, your child will be able to:

- Look at any difficult problem — at school, at home, or in life — and approach it calmly and logically
- Think several steps ahead before acting
- Find mistakes in their own thinking and correct them
- Explain complex ideas in simple steps

These are not just tech skills. These are **life skills** that will serve your child in every subject, every exam, every career, and every challenge they face.

---

### Stage 2 — How Software Is Built (Weeks 5–8)

This is where your child begins to understand the technology they use every day — phones, apps, websites, games — and more importantly, how to *build* it themselves.

They will learn:

- **How engineers design software before writing any code** — the same way an architect draws a house before a single brick is laid
- **Python** — one of the most widely used programming languages in the world, used at Google, Netflix, Instagram, and in hospitals, banks, and government offices
- **How to work in teams** — using the same professional tools that software companies worldwide rely on
- **How to manage a project from start to finish** — planning, building, testing, and delivering a finished product

By the end of this stage, your child will have written their first real programmes and worked on a small software project alongside their classmates. They will understand what it means to be a *software engineer* — not just someone who uses technology, but someone who *creates* it.

---

### Stage 3 — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (Weeks 9–12)

This is the heart of the programme — and perhaps the most exciting part for your child.

Artificial Intelligence is not the future. It is the *present.* It runs the recommendation on your YouTube, the fraud detection on your bank account, the diagnostics in your doctor's computer, and the smart features on your phone. Understanding how it works — and knowing how to build it — is arguably the most valuable skill a young person can have today.

In this stage, your child will learn:

- **What AI is and how it actually works**, in simple, understandable terms
- **How machines learn from data** — the same way a child learns from experience
- **How to build their own AI model** — a real, working piece of intelligence that can make predictions and decisions
- **Computer Vision** — teaching a computer to recognise images and objects (the same technology behind face unlock on your phone)
- **Natural Language Processing** — teaching a computer to understand and work with human language (the technology behind ChatGPT and Siri)
- **How to take an AI idea from start to finish** — designing, building, testing, and presenting a complete AI project

At graduation, every student presents a real AI project they built themselves. This is not a certificate for attending — it is proof of something they genuinely created.

## What Makes IntelliGeeks Different?

### 1. Very Small Classes — Maximum 20 Students

We deliberately limit each cohort to just 20 students. This is not because we cannot accommodate more. It is because we believe your child deserves personal attention, not a seat in a crowd. In a class of 20, every student is seen, every question is answered, and no child is left behind. Instructors know each student by name, understand their pace, and adapt their teaching accordingly.

### 2. No Prior Experience Required

Your child does not need to know anything about computers or programming before they start. We begin from the very beginning and build progressively. Whether your child has never typed a line of code or has dabbled in it before, they will be well accommodated and well challenged.

### 3. Real Projects, Not Just Theory

At IntelliGeeks, your child will not sit and listen to lectures and go home with notes they never look at again. Every module involves building something real. By the end of the programme, your child will have a portfolio — actual projects they designed and built themselves — that they can show to universities, scholarship committees, or future employers.

### 4. Sessions Are Designed Around Your Child's School Schedule

Classes hold **three times a week — Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM.** This is intentionally after school hours, ensuring that the programme supports, rather than disrupts, your child's academic responsibilities.

### 5. Every Session Is Recorded

If your child misses a class for any reason — illness, family commitments, or otherwise — they will not fall behind. Every session is recorded and made available to them. We understand that life happens, and we plan for it.

### 6. A Certificate That Means Something

Upon successful completion of the programme, your child receives an **IntelliGeeks Certificate of Completion** — awarded only to students who genuinely complete the work, sit the assessments, and present their final project. It is not a participation ribbon. It is a credential.

## Frequently Asked Questions from Parents

**"My child is not very good at Maths. Will they struggle?"**
Not at all. The programme begins with thinking and logic, not numbers. Many of our most successful students came in with a fear of Maths — and left with a new confidence in it. The way we teach AI and problem-solving actually *strengthens* mathematical reasoning in a natural, engaging way.

**"My child is already very busy with school. Is this too much?"**
We designed the schedule specifically for secondary school students. Three evenings a week, two hours each. Students who manage their time well — which is itself something IntelliGeeks teaches — find that this programme sharpens their focus and discipline across all their subjects.

**"What if my child tries it and decides it's not for them?"**
We offer a **two-week trial period.** If, within the first two weeks, the programme is genuinely not the right fit for your child, we will process a full refund (minus a small administrative fee). We would rather lose the income than have an unhappy student in our classroom.

**"Is this only for children who want to become software engineers?"**
Not at all. The thinking skills, project management abilities, communication skills, and problem-solving frameworks your child will learn at IntelliGeeks are valuable in medicine, law, business, architecture, journalism, and every other field. We are not training children to write code for a living — we are training them to *think clearly and build things* — which is valuable everywhere.

**"How will I know how my child is progressing?"**
We provide **monthly progress reports** to parents, a mid-programme parent-instructor check-in, and a comprehensive evaluation at the end of the programme. You will always know where your child stands and how they are growing.
